SprintEins Interview Task

Table Management
Requirements

Read the following email conversation between a product owner and a customer, then create a model diagram that you believe would address all the requirements.

Email Conversation

Maria → Tom
Dear Tom, I am reaching out regarding the table management system we discussed. Could you let me know what information you need from me to get started?

Best regards, Maria
Tom → Maria
Dear Maria, thank you for getting in touch! Could you tell me how many tables the taverna has, and whether capacities vary between them?

Kind regards, Tom
Maria → Tom
Dear Tom, we have 12 tables — small ones seat 2–3, mid-range 4–5, and larger ones up to 8. We also have a bar area for waiting guests.

Best regards, Maria
Tom → Maria
Dear Maria, thank you. How far in advance does a reserved table become unavailable to walk-ins?
Maria → Tom
Dear Tom, we block tables 1–2 hours before the booking time.

Best regards, Maria
Tom → Maria
Dear Maria, understood. If a group cannot all fit at the bar while waiting, would they split up or leave together?
Maria → Tom
Dear Tom, they would leave. A group splitting up at the bar would be quite unusual.

Best regards, Maria
Tom → Maria
Dear Maria, noted. Are regular guests' expectations currently tracked anywhere, or is it handled informally by staff?
Maria → Tom
Dear Tom, at the moment it is entirely informal — staff simply know from experience. However, we would like to have that tracked in the system going forward.

Best regards, Maria
Tom → Maria
Dear Maria, for guests who prefer to sit alone — do they have any seating priority, or is it strictly first-come, first-served?
Maria → Tom
Dear Tom, no priority — first-come, first-served. Some solo guests will move themselves if it gets too busy, but that is their own choice.

Best regards, Maria
Tom → Maria
Dear Maria, final clarification — should the system actively enforce seating preferences, or simply reflect the current floor state and let guests act on their own?
Maria → Tom
Dear Tom, simply reflect reality — who is seated where, what is reserved, what is free. Guests manage their own preferences.

Best regards, Maria
