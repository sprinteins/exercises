# Interview Task

The task is to read the following e-mail conversation between a product owner and customer and create a model of that you would implement.
We would like that you bring your solution to the interview.


## Email Conversation

1. **[Maria → Tom]**  
Dear Tom, I am reaching out regarding the table management system we
discussed. Could you let me know what information you need from me to
get started?
Best regards, Maria


2. **[Tom → Maria]**  
Dear Maria, thank you for getting in touch! Could you tell me how many
tables the taverna has, and whether capacities vary between them?
Kind regards, Tom


3. **[Maria → Tom]**  
Dear Tom, we have 12 tables - small ones seat 2-3, mid-range 4-5, and
larger ones up to 8. We also have a bar area for waiting guests.
Best regards, Maria

4. **[Tom → Maria]**  
Dear Maria, thank you. How far in advance does a reserved table become
unavailable to walk-ins?

5. **[Maria → Tom]**  
Dear Tom, we block tables 1-2 hours before the booking time.
Best regards, Maria

6. **[Tom → Maria]**  
Dear Maria, understood. If a group cannot all fit at the bar while
waiting, would they split up or leave together?

7. **[Maria → Tom]**  
Dear Tom, they would leave. A group splitting up at the bar would be
quite unusual.
Best regards, Maria

8. **[Tom → Maria]**  
Dear Maria, noted. Are regular guests' expectations currently tracked
anywhere, or is it handled informally by staff?

9. **[Maria → Tom]**  
Dear Tom, at the moment it is entirely informal - staff simply know from
experience. However, we would like to <!-- not --> have that tracked in the system
going forward.
Best regards, Maria

10. **[Tom → Maria]**  
Dear Maria, for guests who prefer to sit alone - do they have any
seating priority, or is it strictly first-come, first-served?

11. **[Maria → Tom]**  
Dear Tom, no priority - first-come, first-served. Some solo guests will
move themselves if it gets too busy, but that is their own choice.
Best regards, Maria

12. **[Tom → Maria]**  
Dear Maria, final clarification - should the system actively enforce
seating preferences, or simply reflect the current floor state and let
guests act on their own?

13. **[Maria → Tom]**  
Dear Tom, simply reflect reality - who is seated where, what is
reserved, what is free. Guests manage their own preferences.
Best regards, Maria
